About the book

A faculty development book unlike any other on the shelf

A book you read like a novel and use like a manual.

Instead of lecturing teachers about pedagogy, it tells a story. Twelve faculty members, drawn from different disciplines, institutions and stages of a career, arrive at a ten-day residential teaching fellowship led by a former pilot who became a Dean.

Over those ten days, they confront the real questions of modern teaching. The reader learns exactly what the characters learn, and arrives at the same quiet realisation they do.

About the author

From the cockpit to the classroom

Capt. Vaibhav Goutham Suresh writes from a path very few in academia have travelled. He began his working life in the cockpit as a commercial pilot, and went on to work across almost every corner of the aviation industry before turning to teaching and academic leadership. That journey, from flying to management to the classroom, is the reason this book sounds like no other. It is written by someone who has had to learn, unlearn and relearn his own craft in the real world, under real pressure, in a profession where mistakes carry consequences.

He holds a Commercial Pilot Licence, an MBA from IIM Bangalore and Toulouse Business School, and a degree in law, and is completing doctoral research on the Indian airline industry. Today he leads a university school spanning aviation, logistics and tourism, and chairs academic policy across a group of non-engineering schools. His work sits at the centre of how teaching is actually shaped: redesigning programmes and curricula, reforming the way students are assessed, giving real-world experience genuine academic weight, and building the structures through which a large faculty is mentored, trained and renewed. Developing teachers is not a side interest for him. It is the heart of what he does, and this book grows directly out of that work.

What drives him, and this book, is a simple conviction: that teaching in India deserves to be approached with the same rigour, honesty and care as any high-stakes profession. Teaching in Turbulence is his effort to place that conviction in the hands of every teacher who wants to do the work, and do it well.
